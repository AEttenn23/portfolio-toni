## Bio
I've always loved art. I started out drawing and producing music, and while studying the Higher Diploma in 3D Animation, Games and Interactive Environments I realised I could actually make a living from it. It stopped being a distant dream and became my goal.

My profile is generalist: 2D and 3D animation, VFX, tech art and Unity programming. I do my best work in small teams, where I move between the technical and the artistic side depending on what the project and its deadlines need.
